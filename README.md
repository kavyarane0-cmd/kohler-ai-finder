# KOHLER Fit Finder 

### AI-Powered Bathroomware & Kitchenware Recommendation System

KOHLER Fit Finder is an AI-assisted product recommendation platform designed to help users find suitable **KOHLER bathroom and kitchen products** based on their existing fixture, detected issue, available space, dimensions, budget, preferred finish, and style.

Instead of browsing through hundreds of products manually, users can simply **upload or scan an image of an existing fixture**, confirm the detected issue, provide their space requirements, and receive personalized KOHLER product recommendations grounded in the **KOHLER India Price Book 2026**.

---

##  Overview

Choosing the right bathroom or kitchen fixture is often more complicated than simply selecting a product based on appearance.

Users need to consider:

- What type of fixture they currently have
- Whether the fixture needs replacement
- Available room dimensions
- Product dimensions
- Installation requirements
- Budget
- Finish preferences
- Overall style
- Compatibility with other fixtures

KOHLER Fit Finder brings these considerations together into a single guided experience.

The system combines **AI-powered image understanding**, a **structured KOHLER product catalog**, a **rule-based recommendation engine**, and **2D space visualization** to generate personalized product suggestions.

---

# Key Features

## 1. AI-Powered Fixture Scanning

Users can upload an image of a bathroom or kitchen fixture.

The AI analyzes the image and identifies the most likely fixture category, such as:

- Bathroom Faucet
- Washbasin
- Toilet
- Shower
- Bathtub
- Kitchen Faucet
- Kitchen Sink

The system also attempts to identify visible issues such as:

- Leakage
- Visible Damage
- Crack
- Corrosion
- Broken Component
- Replacement Required
- No Visible Issue

The image analysis is powered by **Google Gemini**.

---

## 2. AI-Based Issue Detection
